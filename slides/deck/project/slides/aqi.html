The AQI link disappears once smoking is accounted for

Larger counties have both higher AQI and less smoking (AQI and log population: r = 0.71).

CDC PLACES, 2025 release · EPA AirData annual AQI, 2023 · 35 counties with records

SPEAKER 5 · about 40 seconds Our second question was about air quality. On the left are the 35 counties with records. It looks like worse air goes with less COPD, which is the opposite of what we expect. But look at the dot sizes. Big counties like Wake have higher AQI and fewer smokers. [CLICK] After we adjust for smoking and inactivity, the line is flat. The slope is basically zero, and its interval includes zero. This does not mean air quality is harmless. It means one year of county AQI cannot answer the question. Numbers: r = -0.52; slope -0.104 per AQI point before adjustment, -0.0038 after (95% interval -0.015 to 0.008; F test p = 0.51); -0.0068 among the 23 counties with 300 or more monitored days. AQI and log population r = 0.71. Source: EPA AirData annual AQI by county, 2023.
