County COPD estimates differ more than twofold, and they track all three social needs

CDC PLACES, 2025 release (survey year 2023) · county outlines: U.S. Census Bureau

SPEAKER 1 · about 40 seconds This map shows the CDC's estimate of the share of adults with COPD in each county. Wake County is the lowest, at 4.4 percent. Robeson County is the highest, at 10.3 percent. That is more than double. So a county health planner would ask: which community needs go together with this burden? [CLICK] We looked at three needs: no reliable transportation, no health insurance, and housing insecurity. All three rise with COPD, with correlations around 0.8. But these pictures leave out one thing: smoking. [Speaker 2] will explain how we tested that.
