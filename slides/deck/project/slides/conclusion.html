What these data can say, what they cannot, and what would settle it

Supported

Social needs and COPD estimates rise together across counties (r = 0.78 to 0.84).

With smoking in the model they add almost nothing (R² 0.978 to 0.984).

In the 35 counties with records, AQI shows no adjusted association.

Not supported

A separate link for any one social need: the adjusted slopes are unstable.

Causes, or anything about individual people.

“Social needs do not matter.” These data cannot separate them from smoking.

What would settle it

Social needs measured directly, such as census counts of homes with no vehicle or high housing cost.

A COPD outcome that is not model-based, such as hospital records.

Explicit adjustment for each county’s age, race and income mix.

For a county planner: use these estimates to see where COPD is concentrated,
not to choose which social need to target.

Scope: North Carolina, 2023, county-level modeled estimates
