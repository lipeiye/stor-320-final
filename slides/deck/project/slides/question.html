SPEAKER 2 · about 55 seconds Here is our question. After we account for smoking and physical inactivity, are these three social needs still associated with county COPD? [CLICK] Our method is a simple comparison. First we fit a baseline regression of COPD on smoking and physical inactivity. Then we add the three social needs and ask two things. Does the fit improve? We check R-squared and the error when we predict each county from the other 99. And do the new slopes hold up? We check whether they stay clear of zero with three kinds of 95 percent interval. One caution: this is one year of county data. So we talk about associations between counties, not causes, and not individual people.
